# PrayerLink Brand Guidelines 🌙

## Brand Identity

**PrayerLink** exists to remind Muslims to pause their daily routine and talk to Allah through prayer. The visual identity reflects this mission: peaceful, premium, timeless, and elegant.

## The Logo

The logo creatively combines:
1. **Crescent Moon**: Represents the Islamic calendar and the passage of time.
2. **Mosque Silhouette**: Represents the place of prayer and spiritual connection.
3. **Clock / Time Indicator**: The top golden marker subtly reinforces the importance of praying *on time*.

### Variations
- **Primary Logo**: Full color (Emerald Green background, Gold crescent, White mosque).
- **Secondary Logo**: Monochrome / Line art for high contrast environments.
- **Icon Only**: The emblem without the logotype, used for app icons and favicons.

## Color Palette

The PrayerLink palette is inspired by traditional Islamic art, modernized for digital interfaces.

| Color Name | HEX Code | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Emerald Green** | `#18453B` | `24, 69, 59` | Primary brand color, app bars, primary buttons. |
| **Soft Gold** | `#D4AF37` | `212, 175, 55` | Accents, crescent moon, active indicators. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | `255, 255, 255` | Cards, text on dark backgrounds, mosque dome. |
| **Dark Green** | `#0F2F25` | `15, 47, 37` | Deep backgrounds, shadows, dark mode surfaces. |
| **Warm Cream** | `#F9F9F6` | `249, 249, 246` | Light mode app background, providing a softer feel than pure white. |

## Typography

PrayerLink uses modern, clean Sans-Serif typography (system defaults like Roboto on Android, San Francisco on iOS) to ensure excellent readability and native performance. 

- **App Logotype**: `Prayer` in White/Dark Green, `Link` in Soft Gold. Weight: Bold.
- **Tagline**: *"Talk to Allah Daily"* (Medium weight, tracked out slightly for elegance).

## Usage Guidelines

- **Clear Space**: Always maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the width of the main mosque dome.
- **Contrast**: Ensure the logo is placed on a background that provides sufficient contrast. Do not place the full-color logo on complex photographs.
- **Modifications**: Do not stretch, distort, or change the colors of the logo outside the defined palette.
